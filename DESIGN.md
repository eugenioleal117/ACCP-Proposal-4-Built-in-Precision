# Built in Precision — ACCP / Proposal 04

## Style Prompt
A cinematic architectural exhibition with industrial corporate confidence. Evolve In Good Light's material sensibility into blue-hour concrete, steel, glass and controlled cyan. Use atmospheric full-bleed architecture, oversized compressed sans headlines, fine construction rules and generous whitespace. The house-building sequence explains the craft from survey through handover. Preserve the original ACCP skyline mark. English-facing concept, as in the first three proposals.

## Colors
- Midnight `#081622`: primary canvas; quiet, deeply blue rather than pure black.
- Structural Blue `#214E70`: identity fields, logo application and secondary surfaces.
- Blueprint Cyan `#91CEE3`: restrained contrast, technical lines and short accents.
- Steel `#A9B8C3`: secondary text and material references.
- Chalk `#F1F5F7`: primary text and light surfaces.

## Typography
Inter 400/500 for body and interface. Barlow Condensed 600/700 for architectural display, uppercase, tight leading. Fonts served locally with their SIL OFL licenses. 16px minimum body; 14px controls; metadata 12px.

## Motion
One-time editorial reveals, maximum 600ms. UI feedback 160–220ms with cubic-bezier(.23,1,.32,1). Hero cinematic slow push. Explanatory construction film 18s, fixed camera, deterministic SVG geometry and paused seekable GSAP timeline, rendered with HyperFrames. No looping flashes. Pause/replay and reduced motion with completed-house poster. Keyboard interactions immediate. Pause media offscreen and when hidden.

## What NOT to Do
- No orange, terracotta, amber, warm beige or purple branding.
- No generic glass cards, card shadows, gradients or pill-shaped calls to action.
- Do not replace the original logo or fabricate project credentials, reviews or construction experience.
- Do not claim illustrative architecture is built ACCP work.
- Do not connect the demo inquiry form to a service or persist its entries.
